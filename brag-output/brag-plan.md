# Brag Plan: TypenScrape

## What is this app?
TypenScrape is an intelligent, offline-first pocket sous-chef that turns whatever ingredients are in your pantry into chef-caliber meals using dual-engine AI (Groq + Gemini) and RPG-style dish gamification—with zero ads and 100% offline local search.

## The angle
"Ingredients first. Life stories never."
Most recipe apps force you through 2,000-word childhood memoirs, affiliate links, and full-screen banner ads—and crash the moment you lose signal in a grocery store or basement kitchen. TypenScrape treats meal-prep like an RPG inventory forge that works completely offline.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A giant strike-through of standard recipe blog nonsense: "MY GRANDMOTHER'S SUMMER IN TUSCANY (2,400 WORDS)..." violently slammed away by: "INGREDIENTS FIRST. LIFE STORIES NEVER."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Ingredient Forge: Selecting Beef ("STR Build"), Soy Sauce, Vinegar, and Garlic.
- Dual-Engine AI Chef calculating flavor synergies ("Forging the base ingredients...").
- The Reveal: A Legendary Filipino Adobo with +250 Chef XP, a 4-point Culinary Radar, and instant actionable steps.
- True Offline Badge: "100% Offline. Zero Ads. Zero Cloud Dependance."

## Outro / punchline
"Cook what you have. Level up your kitchen."
TypenScrape logo, PWA badge, and clean call-to-action.

## User flow worth showing
1. Pick random ingredients from pantry (Beef STR Build + Soy Sauce + Garlic).
2. Hit Generate ("Forging the base ingredients...").
3. Unbox a Legendary recipe tier with full cooking steps and instant offline persistence.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Anti-Blog Hook — 3.5s (0.0s - 3.5s)
- Visual: A mock recipe blog article scrolling infinitely with blurry text, banner ads, and "Chapter 1: The Tuscan Breeze".
- Snap cut at 1.5s: Giant black-and-orange sticker slams over it: "INGREDIENTS FIRST. LIFE STORIES NEVER."
- Sequential/interaction: Blog blur -> giant sticker slam with 4px hard shadow.
- Audio intent: Frustrated blog noise cut off by a crisp, punchy impact hit.
- Transition mood: Hard cut on beat (3.52s) -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Pantry Forge — 5.0s (3.5s - 8.5s)
- Visual: Clean tactile Carinderia UI. Grid of ingredient badges pop in: "Beef (STR Build)", "Soy Sauce", "Vinegar", "Garlic".
- Sequential/interaction: 4 ingredient cards pop into the forge slot one by one on beat grid (4.02s, 4.53s, 5.03s, 5.53s). Button flashes: "GENERATE RECIPE".
- Audio intent: Crisp interface click/pop on each ingredient selection.
- Transition mood: Flash transition into Scene 3 (8.52s)

### Scene 3 — The Dual-Engine AI Forge — 3.5s (8.5s - 12.0s)
- Visual: Dark midnight charcoal background (`#1C1917`). Neon Groq Llama 3 × Gemini indicator pulsing.

- Sequential/interaction: Dual engine indicator spinning, particle sparks, progress bar fills to 100%.
- Audio intent: Rising electronic riser / simmer.
- Transition mood: Big drop on 12.02s strong beat -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Legendary Unbox & Dish Mastery — 5.0s (12.0s - 17.0s)

- Audio intent: Triumphant fanfare / bell chime, celebratory brass.
- Transition mood: Smooth slide -> Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro & Offline Badge — 3.0s (17.0s - 20.0s)
