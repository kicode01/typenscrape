## 2. LinkedIn / Professional
Excited to share TypenScrape—an offline-first, dual-engine AI pocket sous-chef designed to eliminate food waste and recipe bloat.

By pairing ultra-fast sub-second generation (Groq Llama 3) with culinary logic orchestration (Gemini) and streaming 16 optimized IndexedDB bundles directly into the browser, TypenScrape delivers instant recipe generation and offline search with zero ads, zero tracking, and zero cloud dependency.

Built with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, TanStack Virtual, and Firebase.

## 3. Discord / Community (Casual / Fun)
I got tired of recipe blogs making me read an essay just to find out how much soy sauce goes into an adobo, so I built TypenScrape.

You literally throw whatever random leftovers you have into an RPG inventory forge (e.g. Beef + Soy Sauce + Garlic = Legendary Bistek Adobo), earn Chef XP, and it works 100% offline with zero ads. Check it out!
